# Customer Account Loyalty Policy

## Bronze Membership
Bronze customers may receive either free standard delivery on the next purchase or five percent off the next purchase. These are the only approved Bronze loyalty benefits.

## Silver Membership
Silver customers may receive either ten percent off the next purchase or free express delivery on the next purchase. These are the only approved Silver loyalty benefits.

## Gold Membership
Gold customers may receive either fifteen percent off the next purchase or early access to the next seasonal sale. These are the only approved Gold loyalty benefits.

## Reward Restrictions
Loyalty rewards cannot be exchanged for cash. An administrator may request one suggestion from the approved benefits for the customer's stored tier. A suggestion is informational only: it is not stored, automatically applied, emailed, or treated as a completed discount or redemption.

## Membership Conditions
The administrator manually selects Bronze, Silver, or Gold. The application does not calculate tiers from purchases, spending, orders, demographics, or behaviour. A valid ISO joining date is required; phone and address are optional profile fields.
